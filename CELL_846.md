# CELL_846.md

## Power Witness: 0.5 V dry, 0.846 V wet

**Photo:** image_8aff3f.png — Klein Tools meter reading 846 (mV), glass table with rain, brown tower + silver bracket.

### What it is

- **Metals giving up ions:** silver finned bracket = anode, oxidizes, releases ions into water sheet.
- **Large positive tower:** brown cylinder = cathode, large surface, energy maker. Bigger positive = more capacity.
- **Dual-pane hurricane window:** glass + air gap = dielectric / capacitor. Holds charge, works wet or dry. Interesting properties alone.
- **Wet vs dry:** dry = humid air electrolyte = ~0.5 V. Rain = bulk salt water electrolyte, lower resistance, adds head = 0.846 V.

### Mass at hand, nothing free

Voltage finite, countable: anode mass, water sheet mass, glass capacitance. When anode consumed or water dries, fadeIter to 0. Same as ditch: power as long as rain falls.

Formula: P = V*I or ρ g Q H — both from same H_m.
